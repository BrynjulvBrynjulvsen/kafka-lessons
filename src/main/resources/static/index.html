LAG / EXPERIMENT

Watch the distance close.

Committed-offset lag = log end − committed offset, per partition. It is an offset distance, not a count of unfinished business operations. Commits can advance in steps.

LAG / INSPECT & CHANGE

Separate work from its checkpoint.

// ExperimentRuntime.kt
Thread.sleep(groupDelays.getValue(group))
kafka.commitSync(completed, ...)

// independently sampled broker offsets
lag = logEndOffset - committedOffset

Change only processing delay at first. Keep the workload and commit policy fixed.

Zero lag means the sampled commit has caught up. It does not prove external business effects completed.
